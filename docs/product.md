# Product Overview

## Goal

Let a payer settle a team invoice with one wallet-approved Devnet transaction that distributes test tokens to several team members.

## Users

- **Invoice creator:** enters the invoice amount, token mint, recipients, and each recipient's percentage.
- **Payer:** reviews the invoice and approves the token transfers from a connected wallet.
- **Recipients:** receive their allocated tokens directly at their wallets.

## Current scope

- 2–5 recipients.
- Shares must total exactly 100%.
- DEMOUSD uses six decimal places.
- Solana Devnet only; wallet pays transaction fees.
- No custody, account system, mainnet support, fiat payments, refunds, or durable database.
